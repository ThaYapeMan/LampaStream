Players

The players LampaStream listens through, and how their lights are timed.

Why it is organised this way (Apple HIG)
One place for one ideaA virtual player and the light timing of the player it follows belong together. One sidebar item, “Players”, replaces two.
List and detailThe familiar Mail/Settings pattern: pick a player on the left, see and change everything about it on the right. On a phone the two stack.
Grouped by role“LampaStream players” are the ones you created. “Other timings” keeps saved timings for speakers no player follows right now, so nothing is lost.
Status at a glanceEach row shows its timing state and delay, with a coloured dot plus text, so you rarely need to open it.
Relationship made visible“LampaStream LMS → follows → Study (Sonos)” explains why the timing card belongs to Study, in plain words.
Destructive actions apartDelete sits alone at the bottom left, away from Edit, as Apple recommends.
